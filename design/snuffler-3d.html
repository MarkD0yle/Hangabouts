Snuffler: 3D look

The same Profile Snuffler, lit like a vinyl toy. No ink outlines: the shape comes from light, shadow and gloss. Same poses, same motion, same icon link.

How the 3D look is made

  • Ink outlines are gone. Every part is lit from the top left and shaded on the far side.
  • Body and head are lit as one rounded form. Limbs, snout, tail and bag are each rounded too.
  • Contact shadows where parts meet: under the head, the arm, the strap, the bag and the snout.
  • Glossy eye with an amber iris. Wet nose. Metal monocle rim with glass reflections.
  • The sign is a card with thickness. A soft shadow under him replaces the white sticker edge.

Risks

  • It's a 3D look, not a 3D model. He can't turn to another angle. A real model needs a 3D tool or a rendering library, and the repo rule is no third-party dependencies.
  • The lighting is recalculated while he swings. Fine here; not measured in the app's web view.
  • Without outlines he has less edge on busy wallpapers. Check him on yours.
  • Idle life still breaks the spec's "completely still" rule. It's a toggle.
