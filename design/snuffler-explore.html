Snuffler: redesign directions

Three ways to make the character read better. Nothing in the app has changed. Each column shows the creature large, then at the size it's really drawn on screen (35%, about 63×79 pt) under an 18 pt menu bar icon, on a light and a dark wallpaper.

What's wrong with the current one

  • Too many small parts. Monocle, chain, satchel, papers, strap, belly, fur ticks. At 35% most of it turns to noise.
  • The snout is the name, and it's the weakest shape. It wraps across the face and reads as a second arm.
  • The "?" is the whole message, and it's the smallest thing. It hangs off to one side on a thin string.
  • No edge on dark wallpapers. Brown outline on a dark desktop disappears.
  • The icon is a yellow blob at 18 pt. Already a known problem.

Profile Snuffler

Real size, light and dark
  • Side view. The long snout becomes the silhouette, and it carries the sign.

All three new directions add a white sticker edge so the creature reads on dark wallpapers. These are sketches to choose a direction from, not final art.
