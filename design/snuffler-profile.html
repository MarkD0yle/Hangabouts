Profile Snuffler: detailed and animated

Direction C, drawn properly. More detail on the creature, and motion with follow-through. This is a design sketch; the app still uses the old Snuffler.

What's new in the drawing

  • Shaded fur, belly tufts, back fur ticks, ringed tail, pink ear and cheek.
  • A fist with knuckles on the bar. Feet with toes.
  • The satchel is back, on his hip, with papers. Gold-rimmed monocle with glass shine.
  • Snout with a highlight, wrinkles and a wet nose. It carries the sign.
  • Failed pose: droopy lid, worried brow, frown, ear down, sweat drop, red "!".

How the icon connects

  • Resting: his head sits in the menu bar.
  • Drop: the head ducks out, the icon becomes his empty burrow, and his arm comes out of the hole.
  • Climb: he goes back up the hole, and the head pops back into the icon.
  • Risk: in the app the panel must overlap the lower half of the menu bar so the arm reaches the icon. Not tried yet.

What's new in the motion

  • Drop lands with a stretch and squash.
  • Follow-through: the sign, tail and ear swing a beat behind the body.
  • Nudge 2 adds a leg kick and a double ear flick.
  • Idle life: a blink, a sniff, slow breathing, a tail sway.
  • Risk: idle life breaks the spec's "then completely still" rule (T2, T6). It's a toggle here. Decide before it ships.
  • Risk: the fine detail is lost at 35%. It pays off at 50% and above.
