Snuffler: real 3D

A real 3D model, drawn live. He has depth, he turns left to right, and the light follows his shape. Drag him to turn him.

Turn

What's different

  • He's a real 3D shape now, not a drawing. Every frame is lit, shadowed and drawn live.
  • He turns: left profile, front, right profile, and anything between. Drag him, or use the slider.
  • He spins in as he drops, and the sign always swings round to face you.
  • Parts have real depth: the snout comes toward you, the tail goes behind, the bag sits on his near hip.
  • Swing, nudge, kick, blink, sniff and the failed pose all still work.

Risks

  • Cost. This draws on the graphics card every frame he moves. Smooth here; not measured in the app's web view or on an older Mac.
  • Softer detail. The fur ticks, bag buckle, papers and monocle chain from the drawn version are simplified or gone.
  • No third-party code is used, so the repo rule holds. But the model lives in shader code, which is harder to edit than a drawing.
  • Idle life still breaks the spec's "completely still" rule. It's a toggle.
